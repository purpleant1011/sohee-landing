# Next.js redesign — direction and provenance

Persuade surface for owners without marketing time. The retained brand is Sohee's cream/plum identity and employee character. The new composition groups work into one interactive workbench and one concrete booking journey, reducing repeated explanations and arbitrary feature-card sections.

- Hero: owner promise plus original brand illustration and daily work note.
- Handoff: owner's goal/criteria/approval flows to Sohee's connected work.
- Interactive workbench: three industries and four work stages; downloadable local text example.
- Reservation: conversation, deposit confirmation, owner's calendar, then two distinct day-before KakaoTalk recipients.
- Learning: six industry examples feed store knowledge, then an explicit general-to-specific example.
- Trust: approval, exception escalation and verified performance boundaries.
- Scope: a price-free responsibility table. No production signup or simulated form.
